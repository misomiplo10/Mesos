01 / THE BASICS

A familiar currency,
rebuilt onchain.

$MESO is a nostalgia-driven Solana token inspired by the little golden coins every Mapler remembers collecting. It is an independent community project. It is not an official MapleStory currency, product, or investment.

01
✦

What $MESO is

$MESO turns a shared gaming memory into a community token. Its identity comes from the grind, the drops, and the feeling of watching a small stack of coins become enough for the next upgrade.

The token lives on Solana and trades against GLD. That pairing is part of the project’s structure: $MESO is measured in GLD rather than SOL on its native market.
